CANS Building — testbed geometry

v9. East to west 175 m, Zone 1 at the east end. North to south 51.56 m. The roof is continuous; the only openings to the sky are the courtyards.

Overall envelope

North to south section

The walkway joins the labs to the zones structurally and is roofed over. The courtyards cut south from the walkway and stop at the south strip.

Courtyards, east to west

S1, S3 and S5 are open to the sky with grass and an external stair tower on the south strip. S2 and S4 are narrow wells under a translucent canopy.

Stairs

The Z1 and Z4 cores sit at the east end of their block, hard against the walkway and flush with the facade. They are open fronted and run the full height, so they are a vertical route between the roof and every floor of those two zones.

Interlinkages out of the building

Two bridges aligned on S2 and S4, out through the labs to the north. Floors two and three only. They give the labs their three-part look from the back.

Walkway

Continuous from Zone 1 to Zone 6 with no breaks. Zone 6 carries on past where it stops.
